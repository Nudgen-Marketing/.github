# Nudgen Marketing

Nudgen LLC is building the future of marketing automation. We create ads, outreach, and growth systems that help teams run more consistent, data-driven marketing with tools that are practical for operators and approachable for developers.

Our focus is straightforward: help teams move faster with better systems, clearer workflows, and tooling that turns repeatable marketing operations into a real advantage.

## What We Build

- Marketing automation systems that support repeatable campaign execution and measurable growth.
- Outreach workflows that help teams manage audience engagement, retention, and follow-up at scale.
- Developer-facing tooling that makes it easier to integrate Nudgen into modern workflows and AI-assisted operations.

- [Nudgen CLI](https://github.com/Nudgen-Marketing/nudgen-cli) for command-line access to campaign and team workflows, and for use with any AI agent (OpenClaw, Claude, and more).

If you are evaluating Nudgen from a technical angle, the CLI and docs are the fastest way to understand how we think about automation, workflows, and developer experience.
